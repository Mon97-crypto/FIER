**Spec:** 3600 × 4320 px · 300 DPI · sRGB · transparent background · designed for **dark tees** (black, navy, maroon, forest). Print at 100%; for S/M you can scale to ~10.5" wide.

**For the printer / RIP:**
- The background is fully transparent. Only the artwork gets white underbase, so no box shows around the print.
- The tiny dark specks (worn vintage texture) and halftone dots are intentional knockouts that let the shirt colour show through. Don't fill or "clean" them.
- Less than 0.4% of pixels are semi-transparent (anti-aliased edges), so no choking is needed for a standard DTF film.
- Vector sources (editable SVG, any tee colour, front/back) download from `/admin` → *DTF print files*.

`*-mockup.jpg` are front/back product mockups (oversized fit) for listings and social posts.
