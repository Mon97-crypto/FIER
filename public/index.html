THE MAN BEHIND THE FIRE

Sathish Kumar

01

The Spark

Sathish grew up on stories told by lamplight. His favourite was always the same: a vanara standing at the edge of an endless ocean, told it was impossible — and leaping anyway. Everyone remembers that Hanuman crossed the sea. Sathish remembered the moment before the leap, when fear was loudest and he jumped regardless.

02

The Smoke

He was told to play it safe. Get the steady job. Don’t gamble on something as “small” as T-shirts. The rejections stacked up like the stones of a bridge nobody believed would float. So he did what the Vanara Sena did: he picked up one stone at a time.

03

The Fire

Nights became print runs. He learned DTF — direct-to-film — because it could carry the colours in his head: saffron skies, burning cities, ten-headed kings, rainbow-winged Jatayu. Every misprint was a lesson. Every tee that made it out the door carried two words he wrote on his wall: fear none.

04

The Blaze

FIER is that wall turned into a brand. Not “fear” misspelled — fear rewritten: Fearless, Intense, Eternal, Rising. Drop 01 returns to where it all began — the Ramayana — because the oldest story we know is still the boldest: an exiled prince, an army of believers and a bridge across the impossible.

Wear it like a vow. Fear none. Burn bright.
— Sathish
