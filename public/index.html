I

The Spark

Sathish grew up on stories told by lamplight. His favourite never changed: a vanara at the edge of an endless ocean, told it was impossible, leaping anyway. Everyone remembers that Hanuman crossed the sea. Sathish remembered the moment before the leap, when fear was loudest.

II

The Smoke

He was told to play it safe. Get the steady job. Don’t gamble on something as “small” as T-shirts. The rejections stacked up like stones for a bridge nobody believed would float. So he did what the Vanara Sena did and picked up one stone at a time.

III

The Fire

Nights became print runs. He mastered DTF printing because it could carry the colours in his head: saffron skies, burning cities, ten-headed kings, rainbow-winged Jatayu. Every misprint was a lesson. Every tee that left the studio carried two words from his wall: fear none.

IV

The Rare

Rare Collections is that wall turned into a label. Small runs, numbered pieces, stories you can wear. Drop 01 goes back to where it all began, the Ramayana: an exiled prince, an army of believers and a bridge across the impossible. Each design is limited to 150 pieces, and when they’re gone, they’re gone.

Wear it like a vow. Fear none. Burn bright.
